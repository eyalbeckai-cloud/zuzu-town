Zuzu Town - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: Zuzu Town does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

What we collect

Nothing. Zuzu Town does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game has no sign-in, no chat, no social features, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools (no third-party SDKs that collect data).

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example where the characters and items are, and the language and sound settings) is saved only on your device, so you can continue playing. It never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is deleted.

In-app purchases

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase that unlocks extra places in the game. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store any payment information. The purchase screen is behind a parental gate.

Children

Zuzu Town is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's Kids Category guidelines and Google Play's Families Policy. Links that leave the app and purchases are placed behind a parental gate.

Internet

The game works fully offline. The only network activity is the purchase process run by the App Store or Google Play when a parent chooses to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
